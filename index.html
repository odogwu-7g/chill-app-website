01 / WHY CHILL

Plans happen.
Chill helps you meet them.

Chill App is a simple way for eligible customers to access a small, approved credit limit for purchases at participating liquor stores.

No awkward IOUs. No guessing. You see your available limit and repayment information before you spend.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

From sign-up to
checkout in three.

A straightforward journey built to keep you informed at every step.

  1. Step 1. South Africa verified.

    Create your account

    Register with your details and South African ID. We’ll use them to verify who you are.

  2. Step 2. R1 000 illustrative approved limit.

    Get your credit decision

    Complete an affordability assessment. If approved, your available limit appears in the app.

  3. Step 3. Scan. Confirm. Chill.

    Scan to Pay in store

    At a participating store, scan the payment code, review the amount and confirm in the app.

Sign up. Get approved. Pay in store.

R1 000 is an illustrative maximum. Your actual limit depends on approval and the credit agreement offered to you.

05 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Need to know?

Here are the essentials before you get started.

Chill App is a credit-based payment app for eligible adults. Once approved, you can use your available limit through Scan to Pay at participating liquor stores.

No. Credit is not guaranteed. Your eligibility and limit depend on identity verification, affordability checks, approval criteria and the agreement presented to you.

You can use Chill at participating liquor stores that support the payment method. The latest store availability will be shown inside the app.

Your agreement and the app will show the applicable costs, repayment amounts and due dates. Review these carefully before accepting or spending.

Chill connects to PayJustNow technology for the Scan to Pay checkout experience. The credit product, payment experience and applicable terms will be identified clearly in the app.
